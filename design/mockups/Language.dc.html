Design language

Minimal and calm: soft colour, frosted glass, one card style per log type.

GLASS

Dock and sheetsWhite 62%, blur 28, saturation 170%. Deeper shadow, radius 32 to 36.
Writing surfaceWhite 50%, blur 26, radius 28. The largest glass area, kept quiet.
Accessible by defaultText 4.5:1 or better. Controls 44 px or larger. Reduce Transparency swaps glass for solid #F4F5FA. Type colour is never the only cue: each type also has its own icon and label.
